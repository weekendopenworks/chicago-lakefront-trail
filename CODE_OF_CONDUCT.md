# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, explain disagreements, accept constructive feedback, and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience. Focus discussion on the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and unwanted personal contact are not acceptable. Do not publish private information or speculate about a contributor’s identity.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where people represent the project. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or block contributors to protect participants.

Report concerns through a private contact channel explicitly listed on the repository or maintainer profile. If none is available, request a private channel without publicly naming affected people or disclosing sensitive details. No response time is promised.
