Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Chicago Lakefront Trail is an independent illustrated guide. It has no accounts, sign-ups, forms or comments, uses no cookies, and shows no ads.

What we process

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, Vercel processes your IP address and request details to deliver pages and protect the service. We also use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It uses no cookies and does not identify you or follow you across sites; it relies on a hash of request data that resets daily. See Vercel Web Analytics privacy ↗.

Photographs and fonts are served from this site, not from third parties. We do not sell or share personal information, and we use it only to run and improve this free public guide, which is our legitimate interest under EU and UK law. Vercel may process data in the United States under the safeguards in its policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal data about you, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. The site is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@chicagolakefronttrail.com. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
