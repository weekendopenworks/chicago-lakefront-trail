Navy Pier

Landmark · Streeterville & Navy Pier · Nearby detour

Follow the pier east and watch the city become a skyline. A Ferris wheel, gardens, and cultural spaces line the way.

The farther east you walk, the more the city becomes a view. Navy Pier extends 3,300 feet into Lake Michigan, creating a long public destination with the skyline behind and open water ahead.

The structure opened in 1916 as Municipal Pier and was renamed Navy Pier in 1927. Its history includes maritime uses, military training, and a university campus before its present mix of public spaces, entertainment, and cultural venues.

The Lakefront Trail passes the landward side of the pier. Treat a visit as a separate detour, with time for the walk out and back. Individual attractions have their own hours and tickets; check the current rules for bicycles.
