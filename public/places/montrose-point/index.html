Montrose Point Bird Sanctuary

Nature · Harbors of Lincoln Park · Nearby detour

Step into the Magic Hedge. The skyline recedes, and the smallest movement in the leaves becomes the main event.

The best way to visit the Point is to slow down. In the brush, a small movement can turn into a warbler; over the water, a distant shape can become something entirely different through binoculars. The city is still close, but it stops demanding your attention.

The sanctuary and adjacent dunes have recorded more than 300 bird species. The 150-yard stretch of shrubs and trees known as the Magic Hedge is particularly associated with spring and fall migration. It offers shelter along a much larger route followed by birds.

Leave the through trail for a quiet walk. Stay on marked paths, avoid crowding wildlife, and let binoculars bring you closer. You do not need a long species list to enjoy a few minutes of looking carefully.

More than 300 bird species have been recorded in the sanctuary and adjacent dunes.

Visiting

Explore on foot, stay on marked paths, and observe birds at a distance.
