Buckingham Fountain

Landmark · The downtown lakefront · Via a connector

Rose-colored stone and sculpted water give Grant Park its grand, lake-facing centerpiece.

Grant Park gives Buckingham Fountain room to be seen. Its large basin and sculptural tiers sit in an open, formal landscape, with the city behind and the lake in front. From the shore, it draws your attention inland.

Dedicated in 1927 as a memorial to Clarence Buckingham, the fountain is part of the park’s long role as a civic gathering place. The surrounding space is worth seeing even when the water is not operating.

Displays follow a seasonal schedule. Check the Park District’s official information before planning around the water or lighting, and reach the fountain using the designated crossings from the trail.

The Clarence F. Buckingham Memorial Fountain was dedicated in 1927.
