Kathy Osterman Beach

Beach · Edgewater & Montrose · On the trail

A generous crescent of pale sand, neighborhood picnics, and a horizon that seems to go on forever.

Begin where the lakefront feels like a neighborhood. Edgewater’s apartment buildings stand behind the park, while the horizon stays wide and open. It is a gentle introduction to a route that will eventually pass harbors, downtown towers, grand museums, and the parks of the South Side.

You may hear two names for the same place. Hollywood Beach takes its familiar name from the nearby avenue; Kathy Osterman Beach honors a Chicago public servant. Both belong to the life of this part of the city.

For a small first outing, connect Osterman with Foster Beach. For a full day, continue south toward Montrose. There is no requirement to reach the other end: the point of a lakefront journey is having room to stop.

Locals also call this Hollywood Beach, after the nearby avenue.
