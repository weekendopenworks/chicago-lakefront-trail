Belmont Harbor

Harbor · Harbors of Lincoln Park · On the trail

Follow the long curve of the harbor past sailboats, lawns, and the towers of Lakeview.

The harbor curve changes the view with every few steps. Sailboat masts gather into a fine pattern against the neighborhood towers; farther around, the open lake comes back into view. It is a landscape made for both movement and lingering.

Belmont is part of Chicago’s public harbor system, but it is also an everyday gathering place. Runners, walkers, cyclists, sailors, and people simply sitting on the grass all share the surrounding park.

Enjoy the harbor from public paths and lawns. Marina docks have separate access rules. Continue south toward Diversey to see how differently a harbor can sit within the same long park.

Belmont is one of the harbors in Chicago’s public lakefront harbor system.

Visiting

Keep trail crossings and dock entrances clear when stopping for the view.
