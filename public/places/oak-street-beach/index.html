Oak Street Beach

Beach · The Gold Coast · On the trail

The Gold Coast meets the sand. Few places put beach towels and skyscrapers quite this close together.

At Oak Street, the separation between city and beach almost disappears. The Gold Coast stands close behind the sand, while the lake stretches away with nothing to interrupt it. The contrast gives this small shoreline a remarkable presence.

The bend brings beach access and through travel together. Watch the line of the shore as it turns toward Streeterville; it is one of the moments that makes Chicago’s geography easy to feel as well as see.

The exposed lake edge responds quickly to wind and waves. Check official notices and on-site signs, and keep out of wave-washed areas. For a calmer pause, choose a place fully clear of the trail.

The shoreline bends dramatically here toward the downtown waterfront.

Visiting

The exposed lake edge can be affected by wind and waves. Follow signs and official notices.
