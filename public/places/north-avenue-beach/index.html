North Avenue Beach

Beach · Lincoln Park & North Avenue · On the trail

The Chicago postcard comes into focus: sand, volleyball courts, a ship-shaped beach house, and towers beyond.

Here, the skyline becomes a companion. The beach opens toward downtown, with the nautical shape of the beach house in the foreground. Sand, pale blue water, and tightly packed towers fit into a single glance.

The beach house’s ocean-liner form gives this stretch a playful landmark. The wider setting connects the lake with the landscapes of Lincoln Park, where the zoo and pond paths make rewarding inland detours.

Busy beach approaches ask for a slower pace. Stop fully clear of the through path to photograph the skyline, and use designated crossings if you head inland. A short visit can be just as satisfying as a long itinerary.

The beach house’s silhouette evokes an ocean liner.

Visiting

Slow down at beach crossings. Swimming and concessions have seasonal schedules.
