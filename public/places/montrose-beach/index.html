Montrose Beach

Beach · Harbors of Lincoln Park · Via a connector

An expansive beach where volleyball nets and lake breezes give way to protected dunes and bird habitat.

Montrose is several lakefront experiences in one place. The broad recreational beach gives way to protected dunes, while a harbor and a bird sanctuary occupy the peninsula nearby. Each landscape has a different rhythm and its own rules.

Arrive for the sand, then give yourself time to look beyond it. The natural areas support birds that find food and shelter along this urban shore. Fences and marked paths help keep those habitats intact.

The main trail lies inland of the beach. Follow the signed connections, leave room for people crossing toward the sand, and check the Park District’s current swimming information before entering the water.

Montrose’s dunes and neighboring sanctuary create an unusually rich urban habitat.

Visiting

Respect fenced nesting areas. Dogs are allowed only in the designated dog-friendly area.
