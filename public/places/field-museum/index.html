Field Museum

Landmark · Museum Campus · Nearby detour

A monumental limestone museum guards stories of the natural world, just inland from the water.

The Field Museum makes a substantial inland pause in a lakefront day. Its broad limestone facade and monumental steps belong to the architecture of Museum Campus, while its collections reach far beyond the city.

The museum’s origins are connected to the collections assembled around the 1893 World’s Columbian Exposition. Today, the building sits beside a lakefront that has itself been reshaped by public works, landscape design, and changing ideas about public space.

Allow several hours if you plan to go inside, and check current tickets and hours. For an outdoor visit, combine the museum’s setting with the campus paths toward Shedd Aquarium and the skyline view near Adler.
