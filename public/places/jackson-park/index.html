Jackson Park & Wooded Island

Nature · Jackson Park & South Shore · Nearby detour

Lagoons, birdsong, and the Garden of the Phoenix reveal a quieter, inland chapter of the lakefront.

A short inland detour changes the scene from open shore to lagoon and woodland. Jackson Park’s paths hold traces of the 1893 World’s Columbian Exposition alongside the daily life of a large city park.

Wooded Island offers a slower pace. The Garden of the Phoenix, also known as Osaka Garden, is a place to walk attentively among stone, planting, and water. It is a destination off the main Lakefront Trail.

Use designated crossings to reach the park and check current garden access. Allow time for the return to the shore; this is a separate chapter in a lakefront outing, not a through connection along the water.

Jackson Park was a principal site of the 1893 World’s Columbian Exposition.

Visiting

Osaka Garden is an inland detour. Follow designated crossings and posted garden access rules.
