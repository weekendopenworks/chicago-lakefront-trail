63rd Street Beach

Beach · Jackson Park & South Shore · On the trail

A generous beach and a Classical Revival pavilion bring a graceful sense of arrival to the southern shore.

The beach house gives the southern lakefront a graceful landmark. Open galleries and balconies turn toward the lake, making the building feel connected to the sand and air around it.

Completed in 1919, the Classical Revival pavilion was designed by South Park Commission architects. Its original uses included changing facilities, showers, and medical rooms; its architecture reflects an era when public bathing became a major civic investment.

Pair the beach with an inland visit to Jackson Park, using designated crossings. Swimming conditions and facility access are seasonal, so check the official beach page before planning around them.
