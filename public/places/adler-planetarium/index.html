Adler Planetarium

Landmark · Museum Campus · Nearby detour

At the end of the peninsula, a copper dome meets the sky and the whole city gathers across the water.

At the end of Museum Campus, Chicago spreads across the water. The lake gives the skyline enough distance to become a single composition, from the dense downtown towers to the buildings continuing north.

The Adler opened in 1930 as the first planetarium in the Western Hemisphere. Its dome makes a fitting meeting point between a view over the water and a place devoted to looking much farther outward.

The outdoor view is a destination in its own right. Museum admission is separate. Take the campus paths out to the point, allow for the lake wind, and return to the through trail when you are ready to continue south.

Opened in 1930, Adler was the first planetarium in the Western Hemisphere.

Visiting

The skyline view is outdoors. Museum entry requires separate tickets and planning.
