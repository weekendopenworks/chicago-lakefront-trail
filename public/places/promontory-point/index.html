Promontory Point

Nature · Burnham Park & the Point · Via a connector

A peninsula of mature trees and limestone steps, with the city a distant silhouette across the lake.

The Point invites you to put the journey on hold. Trees frame open lawns, and the peninsula changes the view as you walk around it. To the north, the skyline is a distant silhouette instead of a wall of buildings.

Alfred Caldwell designed the Prairie-style landscape in 1937. The relationship between meadow, trees, and stone feels natural, but it is the result of careful design on land created along the lakefront.

The historic limestone edge is part of the Point’s character. It can also be uneven, slippery, and exposed to waves. Use open paths, follow signs, and choose a comfortable place away from the through route to watch the lake.

Visiting
